The Challenge

  • 5,000 annual tickets across onboarding, offboarding, and access requests.
  • Every ticket required around 1.5 hours of manual effort.
  • Repeated service desk work slowed access provisioning.
  • Inconsistent role assignment created productivity and audit risk.

The Solution

  • Implemented HR event-driven lifecycle processes.
  • Designed RBAC based on role, brand, store, and access profile.
  • Automated governance workflows and provisioning.
  • Standardized requests, controls, and leaver actions.
