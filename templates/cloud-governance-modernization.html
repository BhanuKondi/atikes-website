The Challenge

  • Scattered visibility across multiple cloud applications and tools.
  • Reactive issue handling led to delays and access risks.
  • Unclear provisioning status for joiners, movers, and leavers.
  • Excess SaaS licenses contributed to unnecessary spend.

The Solution

  • Centralized dashboard for daily operational visibility.
  • JML orchestration with automated provisioning and deprovisioning.
  • End-to-end access request tracking and SLA monitoring.
  • License waste detection through usage and inactivity insights.
  • Trend and forecast visibility to plan capacity and optimize spend.
