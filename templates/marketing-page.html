AGENTIC AI & AI IDENTITY SECURITY

Secure enterprise agent automation with identity governance built in.

Build AI agents that can act across enterprise systems without creating a new identity blind spot. Atikes helps organizations design secure agent identity and access models, connect AI agents to enterprise applications, govern human and non-human ownership, apply short-lived and least-privilege authorization, and establish monitoring, audit, and rapid revocation controls.

Discuss your AI identity strategy

Core capabilities

  • Agent identity, ownership & lifecycle governance
  • Least-privilege, JIT and short-lived authorization
  • MCP & API access governance
  • AI agent onboarding, offboarding and access reviews
  • Human-in-the-loop approvals and kill/revocation controls
  • Audit trails, activity visibility and policy enforcement
  • SailPoint, Okta, Entra ID and security-platform integration
